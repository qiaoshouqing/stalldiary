# Code of Conduct

StallDiary should be a practical and respectful project for independent builders.

## Expected Behavior

- Be clear, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while still being direct about technical problems.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Respect maintainers' time by keeping issues and pull requests scoped.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information, credentials, logs, or user data.
- Spam, trolling, or repeated off-topic comments.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may block participants who repeatedly ignore these expectations.
